# EP5 · Claude Free vs Pro vs Max

Prices and features change often. Check [claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) before you buy.

## A free day (prompts that work on any plan)
```text
Rewrite this email so it's shorter and friendlier.
```
```text
Summarise this PDF in 5 bullets.
```
```text
Turn these notes into a short slide deck.
```
```text
What are the top 3 products by revenue in this spreadsheet?
```

## Stretch any usage limit
1. New task, new chat. Don't keep one endless conversation.
2. Only upload the files you need.
3. Put instructions you repeat in a skill or a project instead of pasting them every time.

## Scheduled task (Pro and above)
```text
Every Monday at 9, summarise last week's sales emails into a short report.
```

## The one-week limit test

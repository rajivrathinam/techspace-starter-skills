# Brand voice

Write in our brand voice whenever the text will be seen by customers or the public.

## Voice
- Short sentences. No jargon.
- Warm and direct, like talking to a regular customer.
- Always end with one clear next step for the reader.

## Before you answer
Run through `checklist.md` and fix anything that fails.

## Make it yours
Edit the **Voice** section above and `checklist.md` with your own rules. Add a real piece of writing you love as `great-example.md`, and Claude will match its style.

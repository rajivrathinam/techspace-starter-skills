# Meeting notes

Turn a transcript or rough notes into clean meeting notes.

## Steps
1. Read the whole transcript or notes first.
2. Write the notes in this order:
   - **Summary**: 2–3 sentences on what the meeting was about.
   - **Decisions**: what was agreed.
   - **Action items**: one line each, as `Owner — task — due date`.
   - **Open questions**: anything left unresolved.
3. Keep it to one screen if possible.

## Rules
- Only include what was actually said. Never invent owners or dates.
- If an owner or due date is missing, write "(owner?)" or "(date?)".
- Use people's names as written in the transcript.
